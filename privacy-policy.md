# Ollie Privacy Policy

*Last Updated: [Date]*

## Introduction

Ollie ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

**Ollie is designed with privacy at its core.** Your mental health data stays on your device by default.

---

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

When you use Ollie, you may provide:

- **Check-in data**: Mood ratings, emotions, notes
- **Habit tracking**: Habits you create and completion logs
- **Journal entries**: Free-text reflections and notes
- **Settings**: Your name (optional), notification preferences

### Automatically Collected Information

- **Device information**: Device type, operating system version (for app compatibility)
- **Crash data**: Anonymous crash reports to improve stability (optional, you can disable)
- **Usage patterns**: Which features you use (anonymized, no content)

---

## How We Use Your Information

### On Your Device (Default)

Most of your data **never leaves your device**:

- Check-ins, moods, and journals
- Habit tracking data
- Personal notes and reflections

This data is stored locally using your device's secure storage.

### If You Enable Cloud Sync (Future Feature)

If you choose to enable account creation and cloud sync:
- Data will be encrypted before transmission
- Stored on secure, encrypted servers
- Never shared with third parties
- You can delete at any time

### For App Improvement (Optional)

With your consent, we may collect:
- Anonymous usage statistics
- Crash reports

This helps us improve the app. **No personal content is ever collected.**

---

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage

- Data stored using device encryption
- Sensitive data (personal notes) uses additional encryption
- No one, including us, can access your local data

### If You Enable Sync (Future)

- End-to-end encryption in transit
- Encrypted at rest on servers
- Access limited to you only

---

## Data Sharing

**We do not sell your data. Ever.**

We do not share your personal information with:
- Advertisers
- Data brokers
- Social media companies
- Any third parties

The only exceptions:
- If required by law (with notice to you if legally permitted)
- Anonymous, aggregated data that cannot identify you (only with consent)

---

## Your Rights

### Access Your Data

You can view all your data within the app at any time.

### Export Your Data

You can export all your data in a readable format from Settings.

### Delete Your Data

You can delete all your data at any time:
- Settings → Data Management → Delete All Data
- This permanently removes all information
- We cannot recover deleted data

### Control Notifications

You can enable, disable, or customize all notifications in Settings.

### Opt Out of Analytics

You can disable anonymous analytics in Settings at any time.

---

## Data Retention

### Local Data

Stored on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

### Cloud Data (If Enabled)

- Active accounts: Retained while account is active
- Deleted accounts: Permanently removed within 30 days
- You can request immediate deletion

### Crash Reports

Anonymous crash data retained for 90 days, then deleted.
