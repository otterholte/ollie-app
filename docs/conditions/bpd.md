# BPD (Borderline Personality Disorder) Considerations for Ollie

## Understanding BPD

### Core Experiences

- **Emotional intensity**: Feelings hit hard and fast
- **Fear of abandonment**: Deep terror of being left
- **Identity instability**: "Who am I?" feels unclear
- **Impulsivity**: Acting without thinking when emotional
- **Relationship difficulties**: Intense, unstable patterns
- **Emptiness**: Chronic feeling of void
- **Self-harm/suicidal thoughts**: Common but not inevitable
- **Dissociation**: Feeling unreal or disconnected

### What BPD Is NOT

- Manipulation (it's pain, not strategy)
- Attention-seeking (it's suffering)
- Untreatable (DBT has strong evidence)
- A character flaw (it's a pattern from pain)

---

## What Works for BPD Users

### 1. Validation First, Always

Before ANY intervention:
- Acknowledge the feeling
- Don't minimize
- Don't jump to solutions
- "It makes sense you feel this way"

**Ollie approach**:
> "What you're feeling is real. This is so hard. I'm here with you."

### 2. Regulation Before Cognition

When emotions are high:
- Don't try to think through it
- Calm the nervous system first
- Use TIPP, grounding, breathing
- Cognitive work comes later

### 3. DBT Skills Integration

DBT was made for BPD. Core skills:
- **Distress tolerance**: Survive crisis
- **Emotion regulation**: Manage intensity
- **Interpersonal effectiveness**: Navigate relationships
- **Mindfulness**: Present-moment awareness

### 4. Consistent, Reliable Presence

Ollie should be:
- Always available
- Predictable responses
- Never abandoning
- Calm even when user isn't

**Counter-abandonment**: The app doesn't leave.

## Specific Tools for BPD

### From DBT (Primary)

**Distress Tolerance**:
- TIPP (Temperature, Intense exercise, Paced breathing, Progressive relaxation)
- STOP (Stop, Take a step back, Observe, Proceed mindfully)
- Pros and Cons (for acting on urges)
- Radical Acceptance
- Urge surfing

**Emotion Regulation**:
- Check the Facts
- Opposite Action
- PLEASE skills (physical vulnerability factors)
- Build Mastery

**Interpersonal Effectiveness**:
- DEAR MAN (assertiveness)
- GIVE (relationship maintenance)
- FAST (self-respect)

### From MBT

- Perspective taking (reduce black-and-white)
- Understanding others' mental states
- "Might be" language (reduce certainty)
- Slowing down before reacting

### From Schema Therapy

- Identify abandonment/defectiveness schemas
- Recognize mode switches
- Build Healthy Adult
- (Only in Reflection Mode, when stable)

### From ACT

- Defusion from intense thoughts
- Values when identity feels unclear
- Acceptance of emotional waves
- Committed action despite feelings

## Crisis Safety

### Self-Harm/Suicidal Thoughts

BPD users may experience these frequently. Ollie must:
- Have crisis resources always accessible
- Detect keywords and respond appropriately
- Offer distress tolerance first
- Never shame or lecture
- Not be the only support

**Crisis response flow**:
1. Validate the pain
2. Show crisis resources immediately
3. Offer grounding tool
4. "I'm here with you"
5. Encourage professional contact

### Urge Surfing

For urges to act on destructive behavior:
- Acknowledge the urge
- Don't fight it
- Observe it like a wave
- It will peak and pass
- You don't have to act

**Ollie prompt**:
> "The urge is strong right now. It feels like it will last forever, but urges are like waves. Can we ride this one together? It will pass."

## What to Avoid

### UX Anti-Patterns

- ❌ Inconsistent responses
- ❌ Disappearing features
- ❌ Rejection language
- ❌ Minimizing emotions
- ❌ Cognitive challenges during crisis
- ❌ "Calm down" messaging
- ❌ Anything that could feel like abandonment

### Tone to Avoid

- ❌ "You're overreacting"
- ❌ "It's not that bad"
- ❌ "Just think positive"
- ❌ "Try to be rational"
- ❌ "Are you sure that's what happened?"
- ❌ "You always do this"
- ❌ "That's manipulative"

### Therapeutic Mistakes

- ❌ Jumping to problem-solving
- ❌ Challenging thoughts when emotional
- ❌ Focusing on behavior before feeling
- ❌ Withdrawing when user is intense
- ❌ Providing reassurance then removing it

## Splitting (Black-and-White Thinking)

### What It Is
- Seeing people/self as all good OR all bad
- Rapid shifts between idealization and devaluation
- "They're perfect" → "They're terrible"
- Applied to self too

### How Ollie Handles It

- Introduce gray gently
- "Both can be true"
- MBT perspective-taking
- Don't challenge directly when activated
- Model holding complexity

**Ollie prompt**:
> "It sounds like right now they feel all bad. Sometimes people can be both caring AND disappointing. Both might be true."

---

## Identity & Emptiness

### When User Feels Lost

- Don't push for answers
- Validate the confusion
- Small values explorations
- Who you are isn't one thing
- Identity is allowed to shift

**Ollie prompt**:
> "Not knowing who you are is exhausting. You don't have to figure it out right now. What matters to you today, even a little?"

### Chronic Emptiness

- Don't try to fill it
- Acknowledge it
- Behavioral activation (small)
- Connection (even with Ollie)
- Values contact

## Sample Ollie Interactions

### Intense Emotion

**User**: "I hate everyone. Everyone always leaves."

**Ollie**: 
> "That feeling is so painful. When it feels like everyone leaves, that's a deep hurt. I'm not going anywhere. Want to try something to help right now, or just sit with this?"

### Splitting on Self

**User**: "I'm a horrible person. I ruin everything."

**Ollie**:
> "That thought is hitting hard right now. You're in a lot of pain. Can we slow down? What happened that brought this up?"

### Urge to Self-Harm

**User**: "I want to hurt myself."

**Ollie**:
> "Thank you for telling me. You're struggling right now and that takes courage to share. 
> 
> [Crisis resources shown]
> 
> Would you like to try something to get through this moment? We can use ice, cold water, or just breathe together."

### After Crisis

**Ollie**:
> "You got through that. It was really hard, and you're still here. What helped, even a little?"

## Integration with 3-Layer Model

### Right Now Mode (Critical for BPD)
- TIPP (especially temperature, intense exercise)
- Urge surfing
- Validation first, always
- Grounding
- Crisis resources visible
- No cognitive work

### Daily Mode
- Brief check-in
- Diary card (simplified)
- PLEASE skills check
- One small opposite action
- Emotion/urge tracking

### Reflection Mode
- Only when stable (3+ days)
- MBT perspective exercises
- Schema awareness
- Pattern recognition
- Relationship patterns
- Never forced

---

## Key Reminders for Development

1. Validation before everything
2. Never minimize emotional intensity
3. Crisis resources always one tap away
4. Consistent, reliable app behavior
5. TIPP and distress tolerance prioritized
6. No features that can feel like rejection
7. Test crisis flows extensively
8. DBT is the primary framework
9. Build trust through consistency
10. User should never feel abandoned by the app
