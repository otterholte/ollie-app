# MBCT (Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy) Tools for Ollie

## Overview

MBCT combines mindfulness practices with cognitive therapy insights. Originally developed to prevent depressive relapse, it's effective for breaking rumination cycles and changing your relationship with difficult thoughts.

---

## Core Philosophy

**Key insight**: It's not thoughts themselves that cause suffering — it's our relationship to them.

MBCT teaches:
- Thoughts are mental events, not facts
- You don't have to believe or fight every thought
- Awareness of patterns can interrupt automatic reactions
- Present-moment focus prevents rumination

---

## The Approach

### Doing Mode vs Being Mode

**Doing Mode** (automatic, problem-solving):
- Trying to fix, change, or escape feelings
- Comparing current state to desired state
- Ruminating on past or worrying about future
- "I need to solve this"

**Being Mode** (mindful, accepting):
- Experiencing the present moment
- Observing without judging
- Allowing what is
- "This is what's here"

**Goal**: Learn to shift from Doing to Being when Doing doesn't help.

## Core Practices

### 3-Minute Breathing Space

**Purpose**: Quick reset, pattern interrupt

**Structure** (1 minute each):

**Minute 1 - Awareness**
- "What's going on with me right now?"
- Notice thoughts, feelings, body sensations
- Just observe, don't try to change

**Minute 2 - Gathering**
- Narrow attention to breath
- Feel breathing in the body
- Anchor to the present

**Minute 3 - Expanding**
- Expand awareness to whole body
- Include any difficulty
- Proceed with broader awareness

**App Implementation**:
- Timed guidance (text or audio)
- Quick access from any screen
- Perfect for Daily Mode check-in
- Use anytime during day

**Ollie prompt**:
> "Let's take 3 minutes. Just to check in and breathe. Nothing to fix."

### Body Scan

**Purpose**: Reconnect with body, practice non-reactive awareness

**The practice**:
- Systematic attention through body
- Notice sensations without changing them
- Curious, accepting awareness
- No relaxation goal

**Duration options**:
- Quick (3-5 minutes)
- Standard (15-20 minutes)
- Full (30-45 minutes)

**App Implementation**:
- Audio guided
- Multiple lengths
- Gentle, slow pacing
- Good for Daily Mode or Reflection

### Sitting Meditation

**Purpose**: Train attention and awareness

**Basic structure**:
1. Breath awareness
2. Expand to body
3. Include sounds
4. Include thoughts/feelings
5. Open awareness

**App Implementation**:
- Guided options
- Timer for self-guided
- 5-20 minute options

### Mindful Movement

**Purpose**: Embodied awareness in motion

**Options**:
- Gentle stretching with awareness
- Mindful walking
- Yoga-informed movements
- Simple standing/sitting transitions

**App Implementation**:
- Brief guided sequences
- No fitness pressure
- Focus on sensation, not achievement

## Cognitive Insights

### Thoughts Are Not Facts

**Key teaching**:
- Thoughts arise automatically
- They feel true but may not be
- You don't have to act on every thought
- Distance creates choice

**Practice**:
- Notice a thought
- Label it: "There's a thought that..."
- Observe it without believing or fighting

**App Implementation**:
- Integrate with thought journaling
- Psychoeducation about thought nature
- Defusion techniques

### The Exhaustion Funnel

**Concept**: When stressed, we drop what "doesn't matter" — often the restorative activities we need most.

**Pattern**:
High stress → Drop hobbies, self-care → More vulnerable → Worse stress

**App Implementation**:
- Track meaningful activities
- Notice what's been dropped
- Gentle reminders about nourishment

### Recognizing Automatic Patterns

**Key patterns to notice**:
- Rumination (replaying past)
- Worry (projecting future)
- Self-criticism (harsh internal voice)
- Avoidance (escaping discomfort)

**App Implementation**:
- Help identify patterns
- Non-judgmental labeling
- Track over time

## Depression Prevention Tools

### Early Warning Signs

**Purpose**: Catch downward spirals early

**Common signs**:
- Sleep changes
- Withdrawal
- Loss of interest
- Negative thinking increase
- Energy drop
- Irritability

**App Implementation**:
- Help user identify their personal signs
- Track relevant indicators
- Gentle alerts: "Some of your patterns have shifted this week"

### Action Plan

**Purpose**: Pre-planned responses to warning signs

**Structure**:
1. What are my warning signs?
2. What helps when I notice them?
3. Who can I reach out to?
4. What professional resources exist?

**App Implementation**:
- Create during stable times
- Review when signs appear
- Not preachy — supportive

### Pleasant Events Calendar

**Purpose**: Ensure nourishing activities

**Practice**:
- Schedule small pleasant activities
- Do them mindfully
- Notice the full experience
- Build positive experiences intentionally

**App Implementation**:
- Suggest daily pleasant activities
- Track engagement (not completion pressure)
- Notice patterns

## MBCT for Rumination

### Sitting with Difficulty

**Purpose**: Learn to be with discomfort without reacting

**Practice**:
1. Notice where difficulty lives in body
2. Breathe with it (not to fix, but to accompany)
3. Offer kindness: "This is a moment of difficulty"
4. Recognize common humanity: "Many people feel this"
5. Give yourself compassion: "May I be kind to myself"

**App Implementation**:
- Guided practice
- Self-compassion integration
- Not for acute crisis

### RAIN Practice

**R** - Recognize what's happening
**A** - Allow it to be there
**I** - Investigate with kindness
**N** - Non-identification (this isn't all of you)

**App Implementation**:
- Step-by-step guided
- Good for processing emotions
- Reflection Mode tool

## Ollie's MBCT Voice

**Introducing concepts**:
> "Your mind is really busy right now. That's okay — minds do that. Would you like to step back for a moment and just notice what's there?"

**During breathing space**:
> "First, just check in. What's here right now? Thoughts, feelings, body sensations. No need to change anything."

**About thoughts**:
> "This thought feels very true right now. Thoughts often do. But a thought is just a mental event — it doesn't have to run the show."

**Pattern recognition**:
> "I notice this often comes up when you're tired or after certain situations. Do you see that pattern too?"

**Gentle encouragement**:
> "Coming back to your breath, again and again — that IS the practice. You're doing it."

## Key Distinctions

### MBCT vs Standard CBT

| CBT | MBCT |
|-----|------|
| Challenge thoughts | Change relationship to thoughts |
| Fix the content | Change the process |
| Doing mode | Being mode |
| Think your way out | Notice and accept |

### MBCT vs Meditation Apps

| Generic Meditation | MBCT |
|-------------------|------|
| Relaxation focus | Awareness focus |
| Escape discomfort | Include discomfort |
| Generic content | Depression-informed |
| Isolated practice | Life-integrated |

## Sample Ollie Prompts

**Starting 3-minute breathing space**:
> "Let's pause for three minutes. Not to fix anything — just to check in with yourself."

**Noticing rumination**:
> "Your mind keeps going back to that. That's what minds do sometimes. Can we try just breathing with it instead of solving it?"

**Pattern insight**:
> "Over the past week, your mood dipped after late nights. Sleep might be one of your early warning signs."

**After practice**:
> "What did you notice? There's no right answer. Just curious."

**Teaching moment**:
> "That thought — 'I'm a failure' — feels like a fact. But it's actually just a thought. They feel different when we can see them that way."
