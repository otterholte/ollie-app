# ACT (Acceptance and Commitment Therapy) Tools for Ollie

## Overview

ACT focuses on psychological flexibility: the ability to be present, open up to difficult experiences, and do what matters. Unlike CBT, ACT doesn't try to change or eliminate difficult thoughts — it changes your relationship to them.

---

## Core Philosophy

**The goal isn't to feel better. It's to get better at feeling.**

Pain is inevitable. Suffering comes from struggling against pain.

---

## The Hexaflex (6 Core Processes)

```
         Present Moment
              |
    Acceptance --- Defusion
         \         /
          \       /
           Values
          /       \
         /         \
   Self-as-Context --- Committed Action
```

### 1. Acceptance
Making room for difficult feelings without fighting them.

### 2. Cognitive Defusion
Unhooking from thoughts — seeing them as mental events, not facts.

### 3. Present Moment
Being here now, not lost in past or future.

### 4. Self-as-Context
The observing self — you are not your thoughts/feelings.

### 5. Values
What truly matters to you, your life directions.

### 6. Committed Action
Taking steps aligned with values, even when hard.

## Tools for Ollie

### Defusion Techniques

**Purpose**: Create distance from sticky thoughts

#### "I'm Having the Thought That..."

**How it works**:
1. Notice the thought: "I'm worthless"
2. Reframe: "I'm having the thought that I'm worthless"
3. Further: "I notice I'm having the thought that I'm worthless"

**App Implementation**:
- User enters thought
- App shows progression
- Not about changing the thought, just noticing it

#### Leaves on a Stream

**How it works**:
1. Visualize a stream with leaves floating by
2. Place each thought on a leaf
3. Watch it float away
4. Don't hold onto leaves, don't push them

**App Implementation**:
- Guided visualization (text or audio)
- Simple animation optional
- Emphasize: thoughts come and go naturally

#### Thoughts as Passengers

**How it works**:
- You're driving a bus (your life)
- Thoughts are passengers
- They can yell, but you choose where to drive
- You don't have to kick them off or obey them

**App Implementation**:
- Metaphor explanation
- "What are your passengers saying right now?"
- "Where do you want to drive anyway?"

#### Sing the Thought

**How it works**:
- Take a painful thought
- Sing it to "Happy Birthday" tune
- Notice how it feels different

**App Implementation**:
- Suggest for sticky, repetitive thoughts
- The point is to change relationship, not mock

#### Thank Your Mind

**How it works**:
- Mind offers unhelpful thought
- Respond: "Thanks, mind, for that thought"
- Don't argue, just acknowledge

**App Implementation**:
- Quick technique for rumination
- Pairs well with "I'm having the thought..."

### Values Clarification

**Purpose**: Identify what truly matters to guide action

#### Values Card Sort

**Common value domains**:
- Relationships (family, friends, intimacy)
- Work/Education
- Personal Growth
- Health/Wellbeing
- Leisure/Fun
- Spirituality
- Community/Contribution
- Creativity
- Nature/Environment

**App Implementation**:
- Present value cards
- Sort into: Very Important / Important / Not Important
- No right answers
- Revisit periodically

#### Values vs Goals

**Values**: Ongoing directions (being caring)
**Goals**: Specific achievements (call mom weekly)

**Values are like**:
- A compass direction (always there)
- Not a destination (never "complete")

**App Implementation**:
- Help distinguish values from goals
- Goals are how we live values
- "What would living this value look like today?"

#### The 80th Birthday Exercise

**How it works**:
- Imagine your 80th birthday
- What would you want people to say about you?
- What mattered about how you lived?

**App Implementation**:
- Reflective exercise for values discovery
- Not for crisis moments
- Part of Reflection Mode

#### Sweet Spot

**How it works**:
- What matters to you that you're not doing?
- Where is there a gap between values and behavior?
- No judgment — just information

**App Implementation**:
- Identifies committed action opportunities
- Gentle, not guilt-inducing

### Acceptance Practices

**Purpose**: Make room for difficult experiences

#### Expansion

**How it works**:
1. Notice where you feel the emotion in your body
2. Observe it with curiosity (shape, size, color, texture)
3. Breathe into that space
4. Make room for it, don't push it away
5. Allow it to be there while you continue

**App Implementation**:
- Guided practice
- Body-based, not cognitive
- Good for intense emotions

#### The Struggle Switch

**How it works**:
- Imagine emotions have a "struggle switch"
- When ON: you fight the feeling, making it bigger
- When OFF: feeling is still there, but no added struggle
- Pain × Struggle = Suffering

**App Implementation**:
- Metaphor to introduce acceptance
- Not about liking the feeling
- "Can you turn down the struggle, just a bit?"

#### Willingness Scale

**How it works**:
- Rate willingness to have this experience (0-10)
- Not wanting it (0), but willing to have it
- Willingness ≠ wanting

**App Implementation**:
- Quick check-in
- Tracking willingness over time
- "What would it take to move one point up?"

---

### Present Moment

#### 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

**How it works**:
- 5 things you can see
- 4 things you can hear
- 3 things you can touch
- 2 things you can smell
- 1 thing you can taste

**App Implementation**:
- Quick grounding for Right Now Mode
- Brings attention to present
- Works well for dissociation, panic

#### Mindful Breathing

**How it works**:
- Focus on breath
- When mind wanders, gently return
- The wandering IS the practice
- Each return builds the skill

**App Implementation**:
- Timed breathing exercises
- Emphasize: wandering is normal
- No failure possible

---

### Committed Action

**Purpose**: Take values-aligned steps even when uncomfortable

#### Small Steps

**How it works**:
- Identify one small action aligned with values
- Do it despite discomfort
- Not about feeling ready
- Build patterns of valued living

**App Implementation**:
- Daily task suggestions
- Linked to user's identified values
- Tiny, achievable, optional

#### SMART Goals (ACT Version)

**Specific**: What exactly will you do?
**Meaningful**: How does this connect to values?
**Adaptive**: Is this workable?
**Realistic**: Can you actually do this?
**Time-framed**: When will you do it?

**App Implementation**:
- Goal setting in Reflection Mode
- Always connected to values
- Flexible, not rigid

## ACT for Specific Conditions

### OCD
- Defusion from intrusive thoughts
- Acceptance of uncertainty
- Values over compulsion
- Willingness to feel anxious

### Anxiety
- Acceptance of anxious feelings
- Defusion from catastrophic thoughts
- Present moment focus
- Values-based exposure

### Depression
- Behavioral activation via committed action
- Defusion from "I can't" thoughts
- Values reconnection
- Self-as-context for identity

### Body Dysmorphia
- Defusion from appearance thoughts
- Values beyond appearance
- Acceptance of discomfort
- Committed action despite urges

### Self-Loathing
- Defusion from self-critical thoughts
- Self-as-context (you're not the thought)
- Values clarification
- Self-compassion as valued action

## Integration with Ollie's Model

### Right Now Mode
- Quick defusion ("I notice I'm having the thought...")
- Brief grounding (5-4-3-2-1)
- Values anchor ("What matters right now?")

### Daily Mode
- One committed action suggestion
- Values check-in
- Willingness practice

### Reflection Mode
- Values card sort
- Sweet spot identification
- Long-term committed action planning

## Ollie's ACT Voice

**When offering defusion**:
> "Your mind is telling you something. You don't have to believe everything it says."

**When discussing values**:
> "What matters to you? Not what you think should matter — what actually does."

**When encouraging acceptance**:
> "You don't have to like this feeling. Can you make a little room for it?"

**When suggesting committed action**:
> "What's one small thing you could do today that matters to you — even if it's uncomfortable?"

---

## Key Distinctions

### ACT vs CBT

| CBT | ACT |
|-----|-----|
| Change thought content | Change relationship to thoughts |
| Challenge distortions | Notice thoughts as thoughts |
| Reduce symptoms | Increase psychological flexibility |
| Feel better | Get better at feeling |

### When to Use ACT in Ollie

- When thoughts are "sticky" and hard to challenge
- When user knows thought is irrational but still hurts
- For OCD (don't challenge, defuse)
- For values-based motivation
- When acceptance > change

## Sample Ollie Prompts

**Introducing defusion**:
> "Sometimes our minds say things on repeat. What if we didn't have to argue with it or believe it? Let's try something."

**Values moment**:
> "In the middle of all this, what still matters to you? Even a little bit?"

**Acceptance invitation**:
> "This feeling is here. Fighting it seems to make it louder. What if we just... let it be here for a moment? Not forever. Just now."

**Committed action**:
> "You said connection matters to you. What's one tiny step toward that today? It doesn't have to feel good."
